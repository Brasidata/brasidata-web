CONSTRUCTION SCHEDULING

From initial planning to execution tracking

Brasidata turns scattered project information into a structured, usable construction schedule. You can work directly in the platform or delegate part — or all — of the work to our AI and our team.

  • Structure the plan

    Organize WBS, activities, milestones, durations and execution sequences into a clear schedule for the project team.

  • Understand dependencies

    Connect activities, visualize impacts between stages and identify what actually drives the project completion date.

  • Track actual progress

    Compare planned versus actual progress, record updates and keep an objective view of construction progress.

  • Use AI when it makes sense

    Instead of starting from an empty spreadsheet, let AI structure, review and update the schedule from your project information.

DATA ENGINEERING

A construction schedule should not live in isolation

Brasidata applies data engineering to the construction industry. This means planning can connect with models, documents, spreadsheets, progress measurements and other project information, using open standards whenever possible.

  • Data connectivity

    We connect information from different sources to reduce re-entry and preserve the technical context of the project.

  • Audit and quality

    We structure processes that validate incoming information and expose inconsistencies before they become rework.

  • Open standards

    We prioritize interoperability and OpenBIM to reduce dependence on any single proprietary tool or format.

  • Queryable information

    We organize data so people and systems can access project information more directly and usefully.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to stop using Excel or Microsoft Project?

No. The goal is not to force your team to abandon tools that already work for you. Brasidata can work with your planning data and help organize the information flow around it.

What is the difference between the four options?

The problem being solved is the same. What changes is how much work you want to perform yourself. With Do it yourself, you operate the platform. With Our AI does it for you, AI directly performs part of the planning work. With We do it together, the Brasidata team joins the process. With We do it for you, we take responsibility for the service.

Is the free option just a trial?

No. Do it yourself is the free way to use the solution on your own. Paid options begin when you want to delegate work to AI or to the Brasidata team.

Is R$ 250 an annual subscription?

No. R$ 250 covers 30 days of access to Our AI does it for you.

How does We do it together work?

It costs R$ 2,500 for 30 days and combines use of the solution with direct support from Brasidata during the planning work.

How much does We do it for you cost?

Pricing is provided on request because Brasidata takes responsibility for the planning service and the required effort depends on the characteristics and complexity of the project.

Will I be locked into a specific software product?

No. Data sovereignty is one of Brasidata's core principles. We prioritize open standards and interoperability so your information remains yours regardless of the tools you choose.
