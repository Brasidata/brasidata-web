WHAT WE DELIVER

Five fronts. One information thread.

Each front solves a specific project or construction problem. Together, they connect what was planned, what was executed, what was recorded and what still requires a decision.

01 / INSPECTION

Construction observed with technical criteria.

Field monitoring, execution records, outstanding items, compliance and support for measurement and acceptance. The goal is to turn inspection into usable evidence — not just another archived report.

02 / AUDIT

What does not add up needs to become visible.

Independent review of documents, data, requirements and technical deliverables. Inconsistencies, gaps and deviations are organized to support decisions, correction and technical accountability.

03 / DOCUMENTS

Technical information should not depend on someone's memory.

Inventory, classification, registration, revision control, metadata and technical archive structuring. Folders, spreadsheets, EDMS and CDEs can remain in place — Brasidata organizes the information so it can be found and related.

04 / PLANNING

Schedule connected to what actually happens.

Schedule structuring and review, physical progress monitoring, deviation analysis and replanning. Documents, records and execution data can be linked to activities to reduce the distance between planning and site reality.

05 / ENGINEERING

Design, review and coordination with information attached.

Engineering design development and review, interdisciplinary coordination and technical support. BIM and open standards are used when they add value to the process — not as an artificial requirement.

FROM FIELD TO DATA

It is not about producing more files. It is about being able to use what already exists.

Technical information is usually scattered across drawings, spreadsheets, PDFs, photos, schedules, models and messages. The work is to turn that collection into engineering context.

FIELD

Evidence connected to what was inspected

Photos, records, outstanding items and observations organized so it is possible to understand where, when and why each piece of information was produced.

DOCUMENTATION

Content identified before it is automated

Classification and metadata help answer what each file is, what it refers to, which revision is valid and how it relates to the rest of the project information.

PLANNING

Schedules with technical context

Activities can be connected to documents, evidence, requirements and design elements so the schedule is no longer an isolated line in a spreadsheet.

HANDOVER

Information that remains usable after the contract

Open structures, interoperable formats and process documentation reduce dependence on a specific software vendor, supplier or individual.

HOW WE WORK

From diagnosis to usable information.

The process starts from the client's actual environment. Rebuilding the entire operation is not a prerequisite.

01

Diagnose

Understand documents, tools, responsibilities, requirements and the points where information is being lost.

02

Structure

Define organization, metadata, relationships, verification criteria and interfaces between people and systems.

03

Execute

Apply inspection, audit, planning, engineering or document management, adding automation where it effectively reduces effort.

04

Continue

Deliver records, data and structures that the team can continue to use, audit and extend.

INFOBIM · BRASIDATA TECHNOLOGY

Information opens in the browser — and remains yours.

When a scope benefits from a digital layer, Brasidata uses InfoBIM to connect documents, models, schedules and other technical data in a simple interface. The goal is not to replace the client's ecosystem, but to create context between information that would otherwise remain isolated.

WHY BRASIDATA

Engineering first. Tools second.

Real process before platform

The solution starts from how the construction project and team actually work, including environments that still depend on PDF, Excel, CAD and field records.

Technology without lock-in

Automation, AI, BIM and software are means, not ends. Whenever possible, information remains accessible through open and reusable formats.

Traceability for review

Work is structured so decisions, evidence, revisions and relationships can be checked later — by people or systems.

Integration instead of replacement
