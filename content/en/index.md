<div class="bd-auto-intro" markdown>
<p class="bd-auto-kicker">WHAT WE DELIVER</p>

## Four integrated capabilities in one information flow.

Each capability addresses a different part of the problem. Together they connect execution, evidence, documents, schedule and engineering information without requiring the client to rebuild the entire working environment.
</div>

<div class="bd-auto-services">
<div class="bd-auto-service">
<div class="bd-auto-service__visual"><span class="bd-auto-visual-label">Field · technical evidence</span></div>
<div class="bd-auto-service__copy" markdown>
<div class="bd-auto-service__no">01 / SITE INSPECTION</div>

### What happens in the field needs to become usable information.

Technical monitoring, records, outstanding items, compliance and support for measurement and acceptance. Inspection stops being a loose collection of photos and notes and becomes traceable evidence.

<div class="bd-auto-service__facts"><div class="bd-auto-service__fact"><strong>Field</strong><span>inspection and monitoring</span></div><div class="bd-auto-service__fact"><strong>Evidence</strong><span>photos, records and issues</span></div><div class="bd-auto-service__fact"><strong>Compliance</strong><span>requirements and acceptance</span></div></div>
</div>
</div>

<div class="bd-auto-service">
<div class="bd-auto-service__visual bd-auto-service__visual--docs"><span class="bd-auto-visual-label">Audit · documents · metadata</span></div>
<div class="bd-auto-service__copy" markdown>
<div class="bd-auto-service__no">02 / AUDIT + DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT</div>

### Find out what exists, what is valid and what does not add up.

Inventory, classification, revisions, metadata and independent review of documents and deliverables. Folders, spreadsheets, EDMS and CDEs can remain in place; Brasidata structures the information so it can be found, compared and audited.

<div class="bd-auto-service__facts"><div class="bd-auto-service__fact"><strong>Inventory</strong><span>what exists and where</span></div><div class="bd-auto-service__fact"><strong>Revision</strong><span>what is currently valid</span></div><div class="bd-auto-service__fact"><strong>Relationships</strong><span>how files connect</span></div></div>
</div>
</div>

<div class="bd-auto-service">
<div class="bd-auto-service__visual bd-auto-service__visual--plan"><span class="bd-auto-visual-label">Schedule · progress · deviations</span></div>
<div class="bd-auto-service__copy" markdown>
<div class="bd-auto-service__no">03 / PLANNING + CONTROL</div>

### The schedule needs to talk to execution.

Schedule structuring and review, physical progress monitoring, deviation analysis, replanning and links between time and technical information. Documents, evidence and deliverables can be connected to activities to reduce the gap between plan and reality.

<div class="bd-auto-service__facts"><div class="bd-auto-service__fact"><strong>Plan</strong><span>structure and logic</span></div><div class="bd-auto-service__fact"><strong>Track</strong><span>progress and constraints</span></div><div class="bd-auto-service__fact"><strong>Replan</strong><span>deviations and scenarios</span></div></div>
</div>
</div>

<div class="bd-auto-service__copy" markdown>
<div class="bd-auto-service__no">04 / ENGINEERING + BIM</div>

### Design and information treated as parts of the same problem.

Design development, technical review, coordination and support with BIM and open standards when they add value. The model does not need to become the center of everything; it can remain one information source connected to documents, schedules and records.

<div class="bd-auto-service__facts"><div class="bd-auto-service__fact"><strong>Design</strong><span>development and review</span></div><div class="bd-auto-service__fact"><strong>Coordination</strong><span>discipline interfaces</span></div><div class="bd-auto-service__fact"><strong>Interoperability</strong><span>IFC and open formats</span></div></div>

<div class="bd-auto-method__step" markdown><span class="bd-auto-method__no">02 · STRUCTURE</span>#### Organize the context<p>Define classification, metadata, relationships, verification criteria and interfaces between people and systems.</p></div>
<div class="bd-auto-method__step" markdown><span class="bd-auto-method__no">03 · EXECUTE</span>#### Work on the actual problem<p>Apply inspection, audit, planning, engineering and automation where each capability genuinely helps.</p></div>
