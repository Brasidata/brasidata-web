## Improve the process without imposing another process

Brasidata develops and applies its own technology to reduce manual work, connect information and make technical knowledge more usable. Technology is introduced where it helps; engineering work remains at the center.

<div class="grid cards bd-cards-4" markdown>

-   ### Automation
    <img src="/assets/images/icon-connection.png" alt="Automation icon" class="bd-icon" />
    Connect files, spreadsheets, documents and systems to reduce re-entry and repetitive tasks.

-   ### Artificial Intelligence
    <img src="/assets/images/icon-think.png" alt="Artificial intelligence icon" class="bd-icon" />
    AI applied to reading, classification, retrieval and execution support for technical tasks, with human validation when needed.

-   ### Structured data
    <img src="/assets/images/icon-standard.png" alt="Structured data icon" class="bd-icon" />
    Information organized so it can be found, related, audited and consumed by people, scripts and agents.

-   ### Interoperability
    <img src="/assets/images/icon-audit.png" alt="Interoperability icon" class="bd-icon" />
    Open standards, BIM and other technologies are used as means to connect information, without mandatory dependence on a specific platform.
